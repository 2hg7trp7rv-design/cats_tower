# Cat's Tower — V2 Product Snapshot

## Product identity

A portrait mobile idle incremental RPG in which four named cats or cat-people automatically fight through an unbounded tower. Shops, delivery, recruitment and reinvestment support combat; they are not the protagonist.

## Preserved rules

- permanent named party size: four
- tap damage to enemies: zero
- auto battle and offline progress are foundational
- no mandatory collect-all, refill or mass tapping
- no player-visible maximum floor
- 10F is the first district boss; 100F is a milestone, not an ending
- one reset system: tower return
- coin levels are unbounded
- character and weapon gacha remain separate
- first copy is functionally complete
- initial ads are rewarded opt-in only
- no initial PvP, guild competition, battle pass, forced interstitial or banner
